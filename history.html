KA HISTORY JONG KA JINGÏASENG SAMLA HYNÑIEW PRESBYTERY (J.S.H.P) SHILLONG

Nyngkong eh nga pynpaw ka jingsngewnguh kaba khraw ïa u Blei ba U la ïai don ryngkat bad ngi ka Jingïaseng Samla ha ki por baroh, bad nga ai khublei ïa phi baroh ha ka kyrteng kaba thiang tam jong u Trai Nongpynim jong ngi u Jisu Khrist.

Ka Jingïaseng Samla Hynñiew Presbytery ka kynthup ïa ki samla, ki hep ki barit bad ki longkmie, ki longkpa kiba na kitei ki hynñiew tylli ki Presbytery kiba shong ba sah hangne ha sor Shillong. Ha shuwa jong u snem 1993, ka ju long ka rukom ba ki samla kiba dei na ki thaiñ kiba hap hapoh ki Hynñiew tylli ki ki Presbytery kiba mynta ba kin don ka mynsiem ban sngew nongwei ban leit sha ki Jingïaseng ha ki Balang hangne ha Sor Shillong ha ki sngi U Blei. Kumta, ka pynlong ïa ki ban byrñia ha kiwei kiwei ki kam ha ki janmiet sngi U Trai, kum ka jingleit shangkai sha ki jaka ïew bad kiwei kiwei ki jaka kiba ïalam ïa ki sha shibun ki jingpynshoi, bad ba kin jngai na U Blei. Kumta, da ka jingïohi jngai jong ki katto katne ki rangbah bad ki samla, ki la seng ïa kane ka Jingïaseng Samla, bad ban pynlong Jingïaseng manla ka janmiet Sngi U Trai. Lyngba kane ka Jingïaseng Samla, ka pynïakynduh lang ïa ki samla na ki thañ hapoh ki hynñiew tylli ki Presbytery kiba mynta, ba kin ïoh ban ïatei ïa ka jinglong ba kynja mynsiem bad ban ïoh ruh shibun ki jinghikai na ka ktien bakhuid ka jong U Blei. Ka long ruh kum kawei na ki rynsan ba kin nang kumno ban pule ïa ka ktien U Blei, ban duwai, bad ban sam ïa ka ktien jong U Blei bad pynshlur ïa ki kumno ban shakri ïa ka kam u kynrad ha kiba bun ki bynta.

Ka jingtrei U Mynsiem Bakhuid ka pynkynduh ïa ka jingshem mynsiem kaba thymmai ha ki khun samla. Bad ka pynlong ïa ki nongkitkam bad baroh ki organiser ba kin trei shitrhem ha ka jingshakri ïa U Blei lyngba ka Jingïaseng Samla da kaba ki ïawer paralok hi ba kin ïa wan sha ki jingïaseng ha ki janmiet Sngi U Trai ha ka jaka ba kin pynlut ïa ka por jong sha ki jingsngewbha jong kane ka Pyrthei. Kumta ka pynlong ïa kane ka Jingïaseng Samla kaba seisoh.

Khnang ba ka jingtreikam jong ka Jingïaseng Samla kan long kaba beit kaba ïadei katkum ka riti pynïaid jong ka Balang Presbyterian ha Ri India, ka Committee kaba la pynlong ha ka 15th December 1993 ka la ïakut ban draft noh ïa ka Constitution jong ka seng, bad la thung da ka Sub-Committee ba kynthup ïa kine ki rangbah ban leh ïa kaba donkam:

  1. Chairman
  2. General Secretary
  3. Rev. P. Dkhar

Ha ka Committee ba la pynlong ha ka 1st May, 1994 la ïakut ban pyntip noh sha ki lai Presbytery Nongstoiñ, Rambrai bad Ri-Lyngngam Presbytery ïa ka jingdon jong ka jingngïaseng samla, Nongstoiñ Christian Youth Organisation (N.C.Y.O) ha Shillong. Namar ka Jingïaseng Samla ka lang nang ïaid shaphrang bad ka jingkhapniah ban pynlong Jingïaseng ha ka ïing sah, la ai jingmut ruh ban pan jaka ban pynlong ïa ki Jingïaseng ha Synod College, bad dei naduh ka 15th May 1994 ba ïa ki Jingïaseng la ïoh ban pynlong ha Synod College.

Ka Re-Organisation Committee ba la pynlong ha ka 17th August 1994, ka pynkylla noh ïa ka kyrteng Nongstoiñ Christian Youth Organisation (N.C.Y.O) sha ka Jingïaseng Samla Lai Presbytery (Nongstoiñ, Rambrai bad Ri- Lyngngam Presbytery) bad la thung ruh artylli ki Sub-Committee – (a) Executive Committee, bad (b) Singing Committee. La ïakut ban pynlong ïa ka Social Meet kaba nyngkong eh ha 5th November, 1994 ha ryngkat ka jingïadon lang kiba wan mih khmat na baroh lai tylli ki Presbytery, kata ki Moderator bad Secretary jong baroh lai tylli ki Presbytery, ki Chairman/Chairperson bad Secretary jong ki Jingïaseng Samla bad Jingïaseng Khynthei jong baroh lai tylli ki Presbytery, Chairman bad Secretary jong ka Jingïaseng Samla Synod bad I Principal jong ka Synod College.

Ka General Meeting ba la pynlong ha ka 26th October, 1994 ka la jied ïa I Chairman, Secretary bad I Rev. P. Dkhar ban jied noh ia ka motto jong ka Jingïaseng Samla.

Ha ka Executive Committee ba la pynlong ha ka 15th July, 1995 la pynskhem ba ka Motto jong ka Jingïaseng Samla kan long “To Beh Sha Pyrshah Ka Thong” ba la pynshong Nongrim na Philipi 3:14. Kumjuhruh la pule ïa ka draft ka rukom pynïaid kam (Constitution) jong ka Jingïaseng Samla ba la pynkhreh da ka Sub-Committee, bad la mynjur ryngkat katto katne ki jingpynbeit. Hadien kata, la rah ïa ka sha ka General Committee ban ïoh ïa ka jingmynjur. Ha ka Executive Committee ba la shong ha ka 9th November, 1995 la ïakut ruh ban phah copy ïa kane ka Constitution jong ka Jingïaseng Samla sha baroh lai tylli ki Presbytery.

Ka General Meeting ba la pynlong ha ka 17th September, 1995 ha Synod College ka la pynmih ïa ki appeal na ka bynta ki para samla kiba don hangne ha sor Shillong ban pyntip sha ki ba ka Jingïaseng Samla Lai Presbytery (J.S.L.P) Shillong ka ju pynlong ïa ki jingïaseng man ka janmiet Sngi u Trai ha ka por 3:30 janmiet ha ki por synrai bad tlang bad 4:00 janmeit ha ki por pynrem bad lyiur. La ïakut ruh ban thoh sha baroh lai tylli ki Presbytery,Nongstoiñ, Rambrai bad Ri-Lyngngam ban ïthuh noh ïa ka Jingïaseng Samla bad ban shimti noh ïa ka jingkitkhlieh khnang ba ka Jingïaseng Samla kan nang kham shit kham shngaiñ, bad ba ki Presbytery kin sngewkhia ban trei hapdeng ki samla kiba wan pule hangne ha sor Shillong.

Khnang ba ki para samla na ki thaiñ jong ki lai tylli ki Presbytery kin tip shaphang ka jingdon jong ka Jingïaseng Samla, bad ba kin wan ban ïasnoh bad ka Jingïaseng Samla, ka Executive Committee ba la shong ha ka 21st June, 1996 ha Synod Office la ïakut ban don noh ki House Visiting Group na ka bynta ban leit ïa kynduh ïa ki para samla bad ki para bangeit na ki lai Presbytery kiba pule bad ba shong ba sah ha Shillong. Ki Group Leader ha ki jaka bapher bapher ki long kumne harum:

  1. Thaiñ Mawprem: – Mr. D. W. Puweiñ, Mr. P. Syiemiong bad I Mr. J. R. Shangrit
  2. Thaiñ Jaïaw & Mawkhar: – Mr. B. Mawsor, Mr. B.D. Marweiñ bad Mr. N. S. Nongrem
  3. Thaiñ Mawlai : – Mr. D. W. Puweiñ, Mr. S. Lyngkhoi bad I Mr. P. Nongphud
  4. Thaiñ Laitumkhrah : – Rev. P. Dkhar, Mr. O. S. Lyngkhoi bad Mr. T. T. Diengdoh

Hadien ba la kha shuh shuh sa 4 tylli ki Presbytery na kine ki Lai tylli, ïa ka kyrteng jong ka Jingïaseng Samla ruh la pynkylla noh sha ka Jingïaseng Samla Hynñiew Presbytery (J.S.H.P.) Shillong, ha ka General Committee kaba la pynlong ha ka 4th November, 2002. Ha kane ka General Meeting la ïakut ruh ban ai sacrament man la ki Quarter katkum ka list jong ki Presbytery. Bad ngi sngew ban ainguh ïa u Blei, bad ban ai khublei ïa ki hynñiew tylli ki Presbytery namar ka jingkitkhlieh jong ki ban pynbiang ïa ka jingwan ai Sacrament ha ki Quarter ba ki dei ban wan ai man la ka snem, haduh mynta. Kumta ka pynlong ia ngi ki samla ban ïoh ïashim bynta ha ka Sacrament ka Jingkhawai U Trai wat lada ngim ïoh ka lad ban leit sha ki Balang kiba ngi don.

KA JINGÏTHUH ÏA KA JINGÏASENG SAMLA NA KI PRESBYTERY:

Ha ka 22nd November, 1996, ka Jingïaseng Samla Lai Presbytery ka la phah ïa ka shithi ka jingkyrpad ban ïthuh noh ïa ka da ki Lai Presbytery, kata ka Nongstoin, Rambrai bad Ri-Lyngngam Presbytery bad lyngba ka Joint Committee jong kine ki Lai tylli ki Presbytery kaba la pynlong ha u snem 1997, ka la phah ïa ka Recommendation Letter sha kine ki Presbytery ban ïthuh noh ïa ka Jingïaseng Samla Lai Presbytery. Dei ha u snem 2000 ba la ïoh jingïthuh lut na baroh ki lai tylli ki Presbytery ïa ka Jingïaseng Samla Lai Presbytery (J.S.L.P.), bad ki la ai ka jingkyrkhu kyrdoh ïa ka Jingïaseng Samla ha ryngkat ka jingwan jngoh jong ki Pastor na baroh ki Presbytery.

Hadien ba ka Nongstoiñ bad ka Rambrai ki la phiah Presbytery, kumta kila mih 7 tylli ki Presbytery bad ïa ka kyrteng jong ka Jingïaseng Samla ruh la pynkylla noh sha ka Jingïaseng Samla Hynñiew Presbytery (J.S.H.P.) Shillong ha ka General Committee kaba la pynlong ha ka 4th November, 2002. Ha kane ka General Meeting la ïakut ruh ban pan ïa ka jingïthuh (recognition) na baroh hynñiew tylli ki Presbytery. La ïakut ruh ban kyrpad ïa baroh ki Hynñiew Presbytery ba kin sngewbha ban pynbna paidbah ha ki Presbytery, District bad ki Balang ïa kane ka Jingïaseng Samla Hynñiew Presbytery (Nongstoiñ, Rambrai, Ri-Lyngngam, Nongklaw, Nongpyndeng, Tynghor bad Umsaw Presbytery) Shillong bad ban kyntu ïa ki khun jong ki kiba sah ha Shillong ba kin wan sha ki JIngïaseng Samla ba ju pynlong da ka Jingïaseng Samla.

Ka Executive Committee ba la shong ha ka 19th October 2003 ka la rai ban rakhe ïa ka jingdap 10 snem ka Jingïaseng Samla ha ka 23rd November, 2003, kaba dei ruh ka sngi ba pynlong ïa ka Social Meet. Ha kane ka jingrakhe jingdap 10 snem, la ïa don ryngkat ki rangbah, ki longkmie bad ki samla kiba wan mih khmat na baroh ki Hynñiew tylli ki Presbytery ryngkat ki kyntien kyrkhu kyrdoh na ki.

Ka jingïaseng samla ka la nang roi katba nang mih ki snem, bad U Blei u la ïalam ïa ki nongïalam ba kin don ka jingkit khlieh bad ban ieit ïa ka kam ban shakri ïa U Blei lyngba ka jingïaseng samla. Kumta, ka don ka jingangnud hapdeng ki dkhot ka Jingïaseng Samla ban ïalap ïa ka Gospel jong u Trai Jisu Shabar jong ka ruh. Ha u snem 2008 ka Jingïaseng Samla ka shim ïa ka sien jam kaba donkam eh, bad kata ka long ban leit pynlong ïa ka Evangelistic campaign sha ka kyntoit mawstep ban ïalap ïa ka khubor ka ktien U Blei khamtam haba ka long ka jaka kaba dang duna kiba la long khristan. La bteng ïa kane ka Campaign ha Mawstep man la ka snem haduh 2010. Kumjuh ruh ha u snem 2015 la ïoh lad ban pynlong ïa ka Evangelistic ha ka ktien Hindi ha khyndai lad, police bazar. Ka jingïaseng samla ka nang kiew ruh ha ka sap ban rwai bad ban put bantem. Kumta, ka jingïaseng samla kaju pynlong ïa ki programme bapher bapher man ka por ban ïaroh ïa U Trai jong ngi U Jisu Khrist lyngba ki jingrwai bad jingput jingtem.

Executive Committee kaba la shong ha ka 22nd November, 2014 ka la ïa kut ban rakhe ïa ka jingdap Arphewsan snem jong ka jingïaseng samla ha u snem 2018. Ka Committee da kawei ka jingmut ka la mynjur ruh ban pynmih da ka Gospel Music Album ban pynsah jingkynmaw ïa kane ka jingrakhe. Ka long ruh ka lad ba kum ki samla ngi ïoh ban sakhi ïa U Trai Jisu ym tang hapoh ka jingïaseng samla bad ki Hynñiew tylli ki presbytery, hynrei sha ka pyrthei hi baroh kawei, lyngba ka sap ban rwai bad ban put ban tem jong ki. Kane ka jingrakhe ïa ka Jubilee ka long ka dak ba ngi pynpaw ka jingsngewnguh ïa ka jingdon ryngkat jong U Blei ha kine ki snem kiba la leit noh namar shibun ki samla ki la ïoh ban mad ki jingkyrkhu ha ka jingim jong ki lyngba kane ka jingïaseng samla. Kum kawei na ki lad ban pynmih fund na ka bynta ka Album bad ka jingrakhe ïa ka Jubilee, ka Executive Committee ba la shong ha ka 22nd October, 2016 ka la ïakut ban pynlong ïa ka Gospel concert ha Anderson Higher Secondary School, Nongstoiñ. Kane ka concert ka la long kaba jop khamtam ha kaba shibun ki mynsiem ki la ïoh ban mad ki jingkyrkhu. Kumjuh ruh ha ka jingïaseng samla ka la ïoh ban pynmih ïa ki calender na ka bynta u snem 2017 and 2018.

KA JINGRAKHE SILVER JUBILEE JONG KA JINGÏASENG SAMLA HYNÑIEW PRESBYTERY (J.S.H.P) SHILLONG

ka jingsngew nguh kaba nyngkong eh ka long ïa U Blei ba U la ïalam ïa ngi ki khun samla ha kine ki snem bala leit. ka Jingïaseng Samla Hynñiew Presbytery (J.S.H.P) Shillong ka la pyndep ïa ka jingrakhe dap arphew san snem ha u snem 2018. La plie ïa ka Jubilee ha ka 10th December 2017 haka jingïaseng kaba la pynlong ha dorbar hall, Mission Compound. la pynshit pyngshngaiñ ïa kane ka jingïaseng da ka jingdon ryngkat ki representative na baroh hynñiew tylli ki presbytery. kumjuh ruh, la pynlait paidbah ia ka Album jong ka jingïaseng samla kaba ai kyrteng "Tang bad Jisu" da i Tbn. N.S.Nongrem, I ba long I General Secretary ba nyngkong eh jong ka jingïaseng samla. Bad la ïoh jingkren na I Rev. T.T Diengdoh, I ba long ruh u wei na ki nongseng jong ka jingïaseng samla ha u snem 1993.

La pynkut ïa ka jingrakhe jong ngi ïa ka jingdap arphew san snem haka 25th November 2018, ha dorbar Hall Mission Compound. Sa shisien pat ngi sngew kmen ïa ka jingdon ryngkat ki representative na baroh ki hynñiew presbytery ha kane ka jingïaseng. I Moderator jong ka Nongstoiñ Presbytery I la pynlait paidbah ia ka Souvenir ban pynsah jingkynmaw ïa ka jingrakhe ïa ka Jubilee jingdap arphew san snem jong ka jingïaseng samla, bad la ïoh ruh ban ai sngwbha kawei kawei ka copy sha man la ki presbytery. Ha ka tei ka sngi la ïoh jingkren na I Adviser jong ka jingïaseng samla, I Rev. P. Dkahr. La ioh ban pynkup burom ia baroh ki nongkit kam kiba nyngkong eh jong ka jingïaseng samla kumjuh ïa I Rev. P. Dkhar I ba la long kum U Adviser naduh ba la seng ïa ka jingïaseng samla.

Ka Jingïaseng Samla Hynñiew Presbytery, wat la bun na ki dkhot jong ka jingïaseng samla ki dei tang ki khynnah pule ki ba wan na ki thaiñ jong ki ne ki hynñiew tylli ki presbytery, hynrei lyngba ki jingkyrkhu U Blei ïa ki kam ki jam jong ki kmie ki kpa ki jong ngi kiba ju ïai kyrshan ïa ngi na ka por sha ka por khamtam eh haki jingduwai ki jong ki, kumta hala i bor i barit ngi la ïoh ban leit ai jingïarap ïa ka NEIMA ka jaka Ri khunswet ka ba don ha ki thaiñ Pynursla ha u snem 2020.

To U Blei kynrad un nang ai bor ïa ngi ka jingïaseng samla ba ngin nang lah ban ïaid shaphrang ban ïai shakri ïa U Blei, bad un don ryngkat bad ngi ka jingïaseng Samla, khnang ba ngin nang lah ban pyndon burom tang ïa U hi, bad ruh Un ïarap ïa ngi ha ki jingpule puthi bad ki kam ki jam jong ngi ha ki por baroh. Haba ngi don hangne ha sor Shillong, jngai na la imei ipa, kiba ha ïing ba hasem, ka Balang bad ka shnong ka thaw ka jingïaseng samla ka lum lang ïa ngi kiba dei na ki thaiñ hapoh ki Hynñiew Presbytery. Kumta, ka pynlong ïa ngi ban ngin sngew kum shipara ha U Trai Jisu. Ka pynlong ïa ngi ba ngin sngew jan iwei ïa iwei bad ba ngin ïoh ka lad ban ïa kren ïa syllok shaphang ki ba tei ïa ka jingim jong ngi ba kynja doh bad khamtam kiba kynja mynsiem. Hooid ka thong jong ngi ka long ban ngin pass bha ha ki jingpule jong ngi, hynrei dei U Trai Jisu Uba ïarap ïa ngi ban poi sha kata ka thong. Dei U Blei Uba kyrkhu ïa ngi ïa ki kam ki jam kiba ngi lah ban ïeng ha la ki jong ki kjat. Kumta, nalor ka por jong ngi ha ki jingpule bad ki kam ki jam, to ngin ai ka por ba kordor eh ban shakri ïa U Trai Jisu bad ban ngin ïai neh bad U, khnang ban ngin poi sha ka ta ka thong ban shongneh bad U ynda ka jingim jong ngi na kane ka pyrthei ka la wai.
