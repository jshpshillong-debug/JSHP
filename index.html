KA HISTORY JONG KA JINGIASENG SAMLA HYNNIEW PRESBYTERY (J.S.H.P) SHILLONG

Nyngkong eh nga pynpaw ka jingsngewnguh kaba khraw ïa u Blei ba U ïai don ryngkat bad ngi ka Jingïaseng Samla ha ki por baroh, bad nga ai khublei ïa phi baroh ha ka kyrteng kaba thiang tam jong u Trai Nongpynim jong ngi u Jisu Khrist.

Ka Jingïaseng Samla Hynñiew Presbytery ka kynthup ïa ki samla, ki hep ki barit bad ki longkmie, ki longkpa kiba na kitei ki hynñiew tylli ki Presbytery kiba shong ba sah hangne ha sor Shillong. Ha shuwa jong u snem 1993, ka ju long ka rukom ba ki samla kiba dei na ki thaiñ kiba hap hapoh ki Hynñiew tylli ki ki Presbytery kiba mynta ba kin don ka mynsiem ban sngewnongwei ban leit sha ki Jingïaseng ha ki Balang hangne ha Sor Shillong ha ki sngi u Blei. Kumta, ka pynlong ïa ki ban byrñia ha kiwei kiwei ki kam ha ki janmiet sngi U Trai, kum ka jingleit shangkai sha ki jaka iew bad kiwei kiwei ki jaka kiba ïalam ïa ki sha shibun ki jingpynshoi, bad ba kin jngai na U Blei. Kumta, da ka jingïohi jngai jong ki katto katne ki rangbah bad ki samla, ki la seng ïa kane ka Jingïaseng Samla, bad ban pynlong Jingïaseng manla ka janmiet Sngi U Trai. Lyngba kane ka Jingïaseng Samla, ka pynïakynduh lang ïa ki samla na ki thañ hapoh ki hynñiew tylli ki Presbytery kiba mynta, ba kin ïoh ban ïatei ïa ka jinglong ba kynja mynsiem bad ban ioh ruh shibun ki jinghikai na ka ktien bakhuid ka jong U Blei. Ka long ruh kum kawei na ki rynsan ba kin nang kumno ban pule ïa ka ktien U Blei, ban duwai, bad ban sam ïa ka ktien jong U Blei bad pynshlur ïa ki kumno ban shakri ïa ka kam u kynrad ha kiba bun ki bynta.

Ka jingtrei U Mynsiem Bakhuid ka pynkynduh ïa ka jingshem mynsiem kaba thymmai ha ki khun samla. Bad ka pynlong ïa ki nongkitkam bad baroh ki organiser ba kin trei shitrhem ha ka jingshakri ïa U Blei lyngba ka Jingïaseng Samla da kaba ki ïawer paralok hi ba kin ïa wan sha ki jingïaseng ha ki janmiet Sngi U Trai ha ka jaka ba kin pynlut ïa ka por jong sha ki jingsngewbha jong kane ka Pyrthei. Kumta ka pynlong ïa kane ka Jingïaseng Samla kaba seisoh.

Khnang ba ka jingtreikam jong ka Jingïaseng Samla kan long kaba beit kaba ïadei katkum ka riti pynïaid jong ka Balang Presbyterian ha Ri India, ka Committee kaba la pynlong ha ka 15th December 1993 ka la ïakut ban draft noh ïa ka Constitution jong ka seng, bad la thung da ka Sub-Committee ba kynthup ïa kine ki rangbah ban leh ïa kaba donkam:

  1. Chairman
  2. General Secretary
  3. Rev. P. Dkhar

Ha ka Committee ba la pynlong ha ka 1st May, 1994 la ïakut ban pyntip noh sha ki lai Presbytery Nongstoiñ, Rambrai bad Ri-Lyngngam Presbytery ïa ka jingdon jong ka seng Nonstoiñ Christian Youth Organisation (N.C.Y.O) ha Shillong. Namar ka Jingïaseng Samla ka lang nang ïaid shaphrang bad ka jingkhapniah ban pynlong Jingïaseng ha ka ïing sah, la ai jingmut ruh ban pan jaka ban pynlong ïa ki Jingïaseng ha Synod College, bad dei naduh ka 15th May 1994 ba ïa ki Jingïaseng la ïoh ban pynlong ha Synod College.

>Ka Re-Orgsnisation Committee ba la pynlong ha ka 17th August 1994 ka pynkylla noh ïa ka kyrteng Nongstoiñ Christian Youth Organisation (N.C.Y.O) sha ka Jingïaseng Samla Lai Presbytery (Nongstoiñ, Rambrai bad Ri- Lyngngam Presbytery) bad la thung ruh artylli ki Sub-Committee – (a) Executive Committee, bad (b) Singing Committee. La ïakut ban pynlong ïa ka Social Meet kaba nyngkong eh ha 5th November, 1994 ha ryngkat ka jingïadon lang kiba wan mih khmat na baroh lai tylli ki Presbytery, kata ki Moderator bad Secretary jong baroh lai tylli ki Presbytery, ki Chairman/Chairperson bad Secretary jong ki Jingïaseng Samla bad Jingïaseng Khynthei jong baroh lai tylli ki Presbytery, Chairman bad Secretary jong ka Jingïaseng Samla Synod bad I Principal jong ka Synod College.

Ka General Meeting ba la pynlong ha ka 26th October, 1994 ka la jied ïa I Chairman, Secretary bad I Rev. P. Dkhar ban jied noh ia ka motto jong ka Jingïaseng Samla.

Ha ka Executive Committee ba la pynlong ha ka 15th July, 1995 la pynskhem ba ka Motto jong ka Jingïaseng Samla kan long “To Beh Sha Pyrshah Ka Thong” ba la pynshong Nongrim na Philipi 3:14. Kumjuhruh la pule ïa ka draft ka rukom pynïaid kam (Constitution) jong ka Jingïaseng Samla ba la pynkhreh da ka Sub-Committee, bad la mynjur ryngkat katto katne ki jingpynbeit. Hadien kata, la rah ïa ka sha ka General Committee ban ïoh ïa ka jingmynjur. Ha ka Executive Committee ba la shong ha ka 9th November, 1995 la ïakut ruh ban phah copy ïa kane ka Constitution jong ka Jingïaseng Samla sha baroh lai tylli ki Presbytery.

Ka General Meeting ba la pynlong ha ka 17th September, 1995 ha Synod College ka la pynmih ïa ki appeal na ka bynta ki para samla kiba don hangne ha sor Shillong ban pyntip sha ki ba ka Jingïaseng Samla Lai Presbytery (J.S.L.P) Shillong ka ju pynlong ïa ki jingïaseng man ka janmiet Sngi u Trai ha ka por 3:30 janmiet ha ki por synrai bad tlang bad 4:00 janmeit ha ki por pynrem bad lyiur. La ïakut ruh ban thoh sha baroh lai tylli ki Presbytery,Nongstoiñ, Rambrai bad Ri-Lyngngam ban ïthuh noh ïa ka Jingïaseng Samla bad ban shimti noh ïa ka jingkitkhlieh khnang ba ka Jingïaseng Samla kan nang kham shit kham shngaiñ, bad ba ki Presbytery kin sngewkhia ban trei hapdeng ki samla kiba wan pule hangne ha sor Shillong.

Khnang ba ki para samla na ki thaiñ jong ki lai tylli ki Presbytery kin tip shaphang ka jingdon jong ka Jingïaseng Samla, bad ba kin wan ban ïasnoh bad ka Jingïaseng Samla, ka Executive Committee ba la shong ha ka 21st June, 1996 ha Synod Office la ïakut ban don noh ki House Visiting Group na ka bynta ban leit ïa kynduh ïa ki para samla bad ki para bangeit na ki lai Presbytery kiba pule bad ba shong ba sah ha Shillong. Hadien ba la kha shuh shuh sa 4 tylli ki Presbytery na kine ki Lai tylli, ïa ka kyrteng jong ka Jingïaseng Samla ruh la pynkylla noh sha ka Jingïaseng Samla Hynñiew Presbytery (J.S.H.P.) Shillong, ha ka General Committee kaba la pynlong ha ka 4th November, 2002. Ha kane ka General Meeting la ïakut ruh ban ai sacrament man la ki Quarter katkum ka list jong ki Presbytery. Bad ngi sngew ban ainguh ïa u Blei, bad ban ai khublei ïa ki hynñiew tylli ki Presbytery namar ka jingkitkhlieh jong ki ban pynbiang ïa ka jingwan ai Sacrament ha ki Quarter ba ki dei ban wan ai man la ka snem, haduh mynta. Kumta ka pynlong ia ngi ki samla ban ïoh ïashim bynta ha ka Sacrament ka Jingkhawai U Trai wat lada ngim ïoh ka lad ban leit sha ki Balang kiba ngi don.

KA JINGÏTHUH ÏA KA JINGÏASENG SAMLA NA KI PRESBYTERY:

Ha ka 22nd November, 1996, ka Jingïaseng Samla Lai Presbytery ka la phah ïa ka shithi ka jingkyrpad ban ïthuh noh ïa ka da ki Lai Presbytery, kata ka Nongstoin, Rambrai bad Ri-Lyngngam Presbytery bad lyngba ka Joint Committee jong kine ki Lai tylli ki Presbytery kaba la pynlong ha u snem1997, ka la phah ïa ka Recommendation Letter sha kine ki Presbytery ban ïthuh noh ïa ka Jingïaseng Samla Lai Presbytery. Dei ha u snem 2000 ba la ïoh jingïthuh lut na baroh ki lai tylli ki Presbytery ïa ka Jingïaseng Samla Lai Presbytery (J.S.L.P.), bad ki la ai ka jingkyrkhu kyrdoh ïa ka Jingïaseng Samla ha ryngkat ka jingwan jngoh jong ki Pastor na baroh ki Presbytery.

Hadien ba ka Nongstoiñ bad ka Rambrai ki la phiah Presbytery, kumta kila mih 7 tylli ki Presbytery bad ïa ka kyrteng jong ka Jingïaseng Samla ruh la pynkylla noh sha ka Jingïaseng Samla Hynñiew Presbytery (J.S.H.P.) Shillong ha ka General Committee kaba la pynlong ha ka 4th November, 2002. Ha kane ka General Meeting la ïakut ruh ban pan ïa ka jingïthuh (recognition) na baroh hynñiew tylli ki Presbytery. La ïakut ruh ban kyrpad ïa baroh ki Hynñiew Presbytery ba kin sngewbha ban pynbna paidbah ha ki Presbytery, District bad ki Balang ïa kane ka Jingïaseng Samla Hynñiew Presbytery (Nongstoiñ, Rambrai, Ri-Lyngngam, Nongklaw, Nongpyndeng, Tynghor bad Umsaw Presbytery) Shillong bad ban kyntu ïa ki khun jong ki kiba sah ha Shillong ba kin wan sha ki JIngïaseng Samla ba ju pynlong da ka Jingïaseng Samla.

Ka Executive Committee ba la shong ha ka 19th October 2003 ka la rai ban rakhe ïa ka jingdap 10 snem ka Jingïaseng Samla ha ka 23rd November, 2003, kaba dei ruh ka sngi ba pynlong ïa ka Social Meet. Ha kane ka jingrakhe jingdap 10 snem, la ïa don ryngkat ki rangbah, ki longkmie bad ki samla kiba wan mih khmat na baroh ki Hynñiew tylli ki Presbytery ryngkat ki kyntien kyrkhu kyrdoh na ki.
